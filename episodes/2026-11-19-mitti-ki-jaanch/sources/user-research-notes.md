Verified Details for [VERIFY] Items

• Scene 6 — Parameters tested ([VERIFY: जांच में कौन-कौन से तत्व शामिल हैं]):
	• Verified Fact: Under the Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme, 12 parameters are tested:
		1. Macro & Secondary Nutrients (4): Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), and Sulphur (S).
		2. Micro-nutrients (5): Zinc (Zn), Iron (Fe), Copper (Cu), Manganese (Mn), and Boron (Bo).
		3. Physical/Chemical Parameters (3): Soil pH, Electrical Conductivity (EC), and Organic Carbon (OC).
	• Suggested Script Fix: "जांच में कुल 12 चीज़ें देखी जाती हैं—नाइट्रोजन, फॉस्फोरस, पोटाश और सल्फर जैसे मुख्य तत्व, मिट्टी का pH, जैविक कार्बन, और ज़िंक, आयरन, बोरॉन जैसे 5 सूक्ष्म पोषक तत्व।"
• Scene 8 — Best time to collect samples ([VERIFY: नमूना लेने का सही समय, रबी के लिए]):
	• Verified Fact: According to the Soil Health Card FAQ and ICAR guidelines, samples should be taken after harvesting the standing crop (Rabi or Kharif) and at least 2 to 4 weeks (20–30 days) before sowing the next crop, when the field is empty. For Rabi crops, this means after Kharif harvest (September–October) before Rabi sowing.
	• Suggested Script Fix: "नमूना लेने का सही समय है फसल कटने के बाद और अगली बुवाई से कम से कम 20 से 30 दिन पहले, जब खेत खाली हो।"
• Scene 9 — Gap after fertilizer application ([VERIFY: खाद डालने के कितने दिन बाद नमूना लें]):
	• Verified Fact: Standard soil sampling manuals specify waiting at least 30 days (1 month) after applying chemical fertilizers or organic manure before collecting a sample.
	• Suggested Script Fix: "जिस खेत में हाल ही में खाद या गोबर डाला हो, वहां कम से कम 30 दिन बाद ही नमूना लें।"
• Scene 10 — Number of spots in a zig-zag pattern ([VERIFY: 8 से 10]):
	• Verified Fact: ICAR soil sampling instructions recommend collecting 10 to 15 sub-samples (spots) in a zig-zag pattern per uniform field unit (up to 1–2 hectares). (Note: 8 to 10 is acceptable for small plots, though 10 से 15 is the standard ICAR figure).
	• Suggested Script Fix: Replace 8 से 10 with 10 से 15.
• Scene 11 — Pit depth ([VERIFY: 15 सेंटीमीटर, यानी आधा फुट]):
	• Verified Fact: 15 सेंटीमीटर (6 इंच / आधा फुट) is exact and accurate for cereals, pulses, vegetables, and other shallow-rooted seasonal crops per ICAR guidelines and the Soil Health Card Manual (15–20 cm). (Deep-rooted crops or orchards require 30–90 cm).
	• Suggested Script Fix: Keep 15 सेंटीमीटर, यानी आधा फुट (remove [VERIFY]).
• Scene 14 — Final sample weight ([VERIFY: आधा किलो]):
	• Verified Fact: आधा किलो (500 ग्राम) is accurate. Standard agricultural university & ICAR manuals state reducing the composite soil via quartering until 500 grams (0.5 kg) remains.
	• Suggested Script Fix: Keep आधा किलो (500 ग्राम) (remove [VERIFY]).
• Scene 16 — Where to submit & SHC Scheme Fee ([VERIFY: कहां जमा करें] & [VERIFY: सॉइल हेल्थ कार्ड योजना की मौजूदा जानकारी और जांच की फीस]):
	• Verified Fact:
		• Where to submit: Farmers can submit samples to their nearest Government Soil Testing Laboratory (STL), Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), Block/District Agriculture Office, or through village-level agriculture staff/Krishi Sakhi.
		• Current Scheme & Fee: Under the Soil Health Card Scheme (now integrated under Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana — PM-RKVY), testing and card generation are completely free of cost for farmers. (The government bears the ₹300/sample operational cost). Farmers receive an SMS alert and can download the card from soilhealth.dac.gov.in. Note: Private labs or extra tests outside the scheme cycle may charge a nominal fee.
	• Suggested Script Fix: "नमूना अपने नज़दीकी सरकारी मिट्टी जांच प्रयोगशाला, कृषि विज्ञान केंद्र (KVK) या ब्लॉक कृषि कार्यालय में जमा करें। भारत सरकार की सॉइल हेल्थ कार्ड योजना के तहत किसानों के लिए यह जांच बिल्कुल मुफ़्त है, और रिपोर्ट SMS या पोर्टल से भी डाउनलोड की जा सकती है।"
• Scene 22 — Frequency of testing ([VERIFY: हर 2 से 3 साल में]):
	• Verified Fact: हर 2 से 3 साल में is accurate. The Ministry of Agriculture / PIB issues Soil Health Cards on a 2 to 3-year cycle (currently a 3-year cycle for general holdings, and annually only for intensive/problematic soils).
	• Suggested Script Fix: Keep हर 2 से 3 साल में (remove [VERIFY]).
